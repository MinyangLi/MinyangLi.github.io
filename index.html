Selected Projects

Multi-object Image Editing
Developed VGFE, an inversion-free flow-based image editing framework that uses VQA-guided editing strength search to automatically select optimal edit strength while reducing computational overhead. Extended the method to multi-object editing by designing a reassembly and re-editing pipeline tailored for inversion-free diffusion/flow models. (Report)
Skeleton-adhered Style Transfer for Chinese Characters
Developed CalliGen, a sketch-based Chinese calligraphy generation system that transforms rough handwritten skeletons into stylized calligraphic characters while preserving users' structural intent. Built a large-scale paired dataset of Chinese characters and skeletons. Designed an interactive prototype supporting sketch editing, style selection, and real-time generation. (Report) (Code) (Demo video)
Fact-Aware Consistency Scoring for Model-Generated Answers
To address the limitations of global embedding similarity in detecting entity, date, number, and span-level errors, proposed FS-BGE, a BGE-M3 ColBERT-based similarity score with factual token reweighting and answer-span mismatch penalties, and further explored bidirectional NLI-based entailment scoring. (Report) (Code)
