Minyang Li is an undergraduate student in Artificial Intelligence at The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou). His research interests focus on how reward or preference signals, test-time feedback, and trajectory-level guidance can be used to improve the controllability, reliability, and alignment of diffusion-based generative models. He has worked on high-resolution image synthesis, computational hydrology, robotic manipulation, image editing, Chinese character style transfer, and fact-aware consistency scoring.
